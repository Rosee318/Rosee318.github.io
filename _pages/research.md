My research sits at the intersection of information systems, online platforms, and user behavior. It spans four interconnected themes.

---

## 1. Open Collaboration & Open Source Software
### 개방형 협업과 오픈소스 소프트웨어

How do people create value together in open, voluntary settings? I study the governance, leadership, and knowledge dynamics of open collaboration — with a focus on open source software (OSS) development communities. This line of work examines how project owners and leaders shape outcomes, how developers explore and exploit knowledge, how openness and network structure drive innovation performance, and how OSS participation reshapes developers' careers.

**Representative work**
- Who Is the Key Man in an Open Community? Based on Organizational Learning Theory with Structural Holes and Strength of Ties, *Information and Software Technology* (2026)
- The role of openness in open collaboration, *ETRI Journal* (2020)
- Governance strategies for open collaboration, *International Journal of Information Management* (2017)

---

## 2. Online Reviews & User-Generated Content
### 온라인 리뷰와 사용자 생성 콘텐츠

What makes online reviews and user-generated content useful, credible, and influential? I investigate the drivers of review helpfulness — reviewer expertise and experience, rating dispersion and extremity, and normative social influence — often using large-scale platform data (e.g., the Steam game platform). I also study how content creators shape what audiences choose to consume.

**Representative work**
- Visual and Verbal Fit-Diagnostic Contextual Cues and Review Helpfulness in Online Apparel Reviews, *Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services* (2026)
- How the reviewer's historical ratings influence review helpfulness, *Industrial Management & Data Systems* (2024)
- Who Makes Popular Content? Focusing on the User-Created Content Platform "Roblox", *Entertainment Computing* (2024)
- Does the dispersion of online review ratings affect review helpfulness?, *Computers in Human Behavior* (2020)

---

## 3. The Dark Side of Social Media: Online Behavior, Privacy & Security
### 인터넷 속의 부정적 행동 — 프라이버시·보안 포함

Digital platforms enable connection, but also harm. This theme examines the negative and risky sides of online life — cyberbullying, online sexual harassment, malicious commenting, social comparison and envy — and how design affordances (such as blockability) and individual motivations shape whether people confront or protect themselves. Building on this, I increasingly study **privacy and cybersecurity behavior**: how users weigh privacy in adopting smart technologies, and how identity and motivation drive protective, security-related behavior online. This emerging security direction is being developed together with students in the lab.

**Representative work**
- The Effect of Blockability Affordance on Confrontation against Cyberbullying on Social Networking Sites, *Internet Research* (2024)
- How does live streaming impact media content consumption? The effect of game live streaming on game players, *Entertainment Computing* (2024)
- How people perceive malicious comments differently, *Frontiers in Psychology* (2023)
- Electronic Cigarette Themes on Twitter, *Health Communication* (2020)

---

## 4. Technology Adoption & Digital Innovation
### 디지털 기술 수용과 플랫폼·서비스 혁신

How do individuals and organizations adopt, sustain, and profit from new digital technologies and platforms? I study adoption and continuance of platform-based services (e-commerce, streaming, OTT, metaverse, smart home, fintech), technology forecasting, and the drivers of entrepreneurial intention and innovation performance.

**Representative work**
- Drivers of Multihoming in OTT Services: Parasocial Interaction and Technology-Enabled Flow, *Asia Pacific Journal of Information Systems* (2026)
- Factors Influencing Entrepreneurial Intention, *Journal of Open Innovation: Technology, Market, and Complexity* (2022)
- How much are sellers willing to pay for the features offered by their e-commerce platform?, *Telecommunications Policy* (2019)
