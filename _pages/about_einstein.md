Saerom Lee is an Associate Professor in the School of Business Administration at Kyungpook National University. Her research focuses on Management Information Systems, open collaboration, social media analytics, online reviews, cyberbullying and online sexual harassment, innovation performance, and AI-based services and user behavior.
